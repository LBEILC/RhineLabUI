# Example research note

This is sample documentation, not a report of measured performance. It creates
a second collection to demonstrate navigation between folders of unequal sizes.

## A calculation

For a hypothetical sequence of $n$ frame times, the average is:

$$
\bar{t} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} t_i
$$
